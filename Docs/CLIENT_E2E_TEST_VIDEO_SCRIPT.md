# Reloved — End-to-end test (video / client walkthrough)

**Site:** https://reloved-digital.web.app  

**You need two phones (or two browsers):**  
- **Account A** = Giver (donor)  
- **Account B** = Claimer (receiver)  

Use **two different phone numbers / emails**. Do not claim your own item.

---

## Quick map

| Step | Who | What |
|------|-----|------|
| 1 | Account A | Sign up / log in |
| 2 | Account A | Drop an item |
| 3 | Admin | Approve the item |
| 4 | Account B | Sign up / log in → claim the item |
| 5 | Account A | Accept the claim |
| 6 | Account A | Book Borzo/Porter (or copy claimer details) |
| 7 | Account A | Mark **Handed over** |
| 8 | Account B | Confirm **Received** |

---

## Step-by-step

### Step 1 — Giver logs in (Account A)
1. Open https://reloved-digital.web.app  
2. Tap **Account** (or profile)  
3. **Sign up / log in** with phone OTP or email  
4. Complete profile if asked (name, address / building)

> **On camera:** “This is the giver account — the person giving clothes.”

---

### Step 2 — Drop an item (Account A)
1. Tap **Drop an item**  
2. Add clear photos of the item  
3. Fill title / details (follow the form)  
4. Choose handover style if asked (courier / gate pickup)  
5. Submit  

> **On camera:** “Item is submitted. It waits for Reloved admin approval before it goes live on the Wall.”

---

### Step 3 — Admin approves the item
1. Open **Admin** (Reloved team)  
2. Find the new donation / item  
3. **Approve** so it appears on the **Wall of Kindness**

> **On camera:** “Admin approved — now anyone can claim it from the Wall.”  
> *(If filming without admin UI, say: “Admin has approved this item; it’s live on the Wall.”)*

---

### Step 4 — Claimer logs in and claims (Account B)
1. On the **second phone/browser**, open the site  
2. **Sign up / log in** as Account B (different number/email)  
3. Open **Wall of Kindness**  
4. Open the item Account A dropped  
5. Tap **Claim** / request the item  
6. Save delivery **building / landmark** when asked  

> **On camera:** “Second account claims the item. Now we wait for the giver to Accept.”

---

### Step 5 — Giver accepts the claim (Account A)
1. Back on Account A → **Account → Giving** (or open the gift)  
2. See the request: **Someone wants to Relove…**  
3. Tap **Accept**  
   - Or **Decline** only if you can’t do the match (distance / timing — you don’t need to know the claimer personally)

> **On camera:** “Giver Accepts — match is confirmed. Status becomes Matched.”

---

### Step 6 — Book courier / fallback details (Account A)
On the **Matched** card:

1. See **claimer details** (name, address, phone) — **tap to copy** (or **Copy all**)  
2. Tap **Open Borzo** or **Open Porter**  
   - Addresses copy to clipboard  
   - App opens on phone  
   - Paste pickup + drop in the app and book (user pays in app)  
3. Or use the copied claimer details with any delivery partner as **fallback**

> **On camera:**  
> “Giver can open Borzo or Porter — details copy automatically.  
> For fallback, name, address and number are copyable so you can send any rider.”  
>  
> **Giver:** leave the bag at the **building main gate** (not flat).  
> **Claimer** can also book from their own claim page if preferred.

---

### Step 7 — Giver marks handed over (Account A)
1. When the bag is with the rider / handed at gate, tap **Handed over**  
2. Optionally message in **Chat with receiver** / **Chat with Reloved**

> **On camera:** “Giver confirms handover. Claimer is notified to confirm receipt.”

---

### Step 8 — Claimer confirms received (Account B)
1. Account B → **Account → Claiming** → open the claim  
2. Tap **Received** (when the item arrives)  
3. Status becomes **Reloved** ❤️  

> **On camera:** “Claimer confirms Received — handover complete. That’s a full Reloved cycle.”

## Tips for a clean video

1. Film **Account A** steps 1–3, then cut to “Admin approved.”  
2. Film **Account B** claim (step 4).  
3. Back to **Account A** Accept + Borzo/Porter + Handed over (steps 5–7).  
4. Back to **Account B** Received (step 8).  
5. Keep each step under ~30–45 seconds of screen time.  
6. Say the step title out loud before tapping.
