## 1. What Reloved is

Reloved is a **digital Wall of Kindness** that makes giving quality preloved items feel simple — while **every item remains completely free**.

Reloved is a **platform that facilitates** the giving and claiming of preloved items. Reloved is **not** the owner, seller, buyer, or guarantor of any item. We help catalogue items, connect people, and coordinate handovers — including with verified community partners (e.g. school / NGO / ashram) where applicable.

## 6. How Give & Claim work (product rules from client feedback)

### Give

- Audience tags: **Men / Women / Girls / Boys** (Unisex where relevant, e.g. bags).
- Categories: **Outerwear | Tops | Bottoms | Kicks | Bags | Accessories**.
- Apparel sizes: **XS | S | M | L | XL | Oversized**.
- Girls/Boys: age bands on Give (Zara/Nike-style bands used in product; exact bands pending final client confirm).
- Single or bulk upload; user groups photos as **Same item / New item**.
- Optional Wall of Love recognition: name, username, or anonymous.
- Photos may be reviewed and shown on the Wall after approval.

### Claim

- Individual claims are reviewed by the Reloved team (typically **24–48 hours**) and may be approved or declined.
- A **monthly claim limit** may apply (currently **up to 3 claims per month**).
- Partner / NGO allocations are a **separate** flow from individual claims.
- Claimed / allocated items are removed from active Wall inventory so they cannot be claimed again.

### Accounts

- Some features require an account.
- Sign-in / contact changes may use **email or SMS OTP**.
- We may suspend access for misuse, resale, fraud, harassment, or safety reasons.

### Coming-soon waitlist

- Email **and** mobile are both required; **name optional**; **no OTP** on waitlist.
- After contact, users may indicate interest to **Donate** or **Claim** (preference for launch outreach).
