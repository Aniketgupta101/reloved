Reloved operates a **digital Wall of Kindness** that helps people give and claim quality preloved items **for free** (Mumbai-led pilot). This policy describes the personal information we collect on the Reloved website and how we use it.

---

## 1. Information we collect

Mapped to what the client asked us to build:

### Account & profile

When you create or update a donor account we may collect:

- Name, username  
- Clothing preference (**Men / Women / Girls / Boys**)  
- Mobile number, email  
- Address type, address, pincode  

Contact details may be verified with **OTP** (email or SMS) on the main app.

### Give submissions

- Item details: category (**Outerwear / Tops / Bottoms / Kicks / Bags / Accessories**), size, description, photos  
- Audience / age context where relevant (Girls/Boys age bands)  
- Handover preferences  
- Wall of Love recognition preference (name, username, or anonymous)  
- Quality / free-gift confirmation and Terms acceptance  

Photos may be compressed or processed (including automated assist) so they can be shown on the Wall after review.

### Claim requests

- Name, phone, handover address, optional note  
- Confirmations required by client feedback:  
  - **Personal use / not for sale**  
  - Agreement to **Terms & Conditions** and **Privacy Policy**  
- Claim review status and related ops notes needed to complete handover  

### Coming-soon waitlist (client: email + mobile required, no OTP)

- Email and mobile (**both required**)  
- Name (**optional**)  
- Interest: **Donate** and/or **Claim** (preference for launch updates)  
- **No OTP** on the waitlist form  

### Verification

- One-time codes (OTP) sent by email or SMS when you sign in or change contact details on the main app  

### Technical

- Basic device/browser and usage logs needed to run and secure the service  

---

## 2. How we use information

- To run **Give**, **Claim**, tracking, account, and **Wall of Kindness** features  
- To verify contact details where OTP is required  
- To review claims (typically within 24–48 hours) and coordinate handovers with our operations team and, where needed, verified community partners  
- To show matching / “picked for you” style recommendations based on clothing preference  
- To enforce product rules such as monthly claim limits and removing claimed items from active inventory  
- To contact you about your submissions, claims, or launch updates you signed up for (including waitlist Donate/Claim preference)  

We do **not** use waitlist Donate/Claim intent as a binding commitment unless we later tell you otherwise.

---

## 3. Sharing

- We **do not sell** your personal data.  
- Exact residential addresses are **not** published on the public Wall.  
- We may share limited contact or handover details with verified logistics or community partners **only as needed** to complete a give or claim.  
- We use service providers (hosting, email/SMS delivery, file storage) only to operate Reloved.  

---

## 4. Photos & recognition

- Item photos you upload may appear on the Wall after review.  
- Wall of Love recognition follows the preference you select (name, username, or anonymous).  
- Photos that may include children are handled with care; parents/guardians may give or claim items for children. Broader public use of children’s images beyond operations needs appropriate care/consent.

## 5. Retention & security

We keep information as long as needed to operate Reloved, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes, then delete or anonymise where practical. We use reasonable measures to protect data; no online service is perfectly secure.

## 6. Your choices

- Update profile details in your account where available.  
- Request access, correction, or deletion: [hello@reloved.digital](mailto:hello@reloved.digital)  
- On Give and Claim you must actively accept Terms & Privacy before submitting.  

**Consent model (current product, pending client Q3):** one combined **T&C + Privacy** checkbox. No separate Cookie / WhatsApp / SMS consent screens unless the client asks for them.

## 7. Children

Reloved is for **adults coordinating** gifts. Parents or guardians may give or claim items for children. If you believe we hold a child’s data inappropriately, contact us and we will review promptly.
